A still place — sit for a moment

As a deep lake is clear and still, so the wise grow calm on hearing the teaching.
Dhammapada 82

The figure and the light around him swell and settle to pace your breathing: breathe in for four seconds as they grow, and out for six as they settle. It repeats until you leave.

Breathe in

Why this page exists

The rest of this site is compilers, packet captures and security write-ups. This one is not. It is here because a page can be a quiet room, and quiet rooms are in short supply.

Every verse shown here is from the Pali Canon, with its citation attached. That matters more than it might seem: a great many sayings passed around as the Buddha’s were never his. “Holding on to anger is like grasping a hot coal”, “what you think, you become”, “peace comes from within” — all modern inventions. If a line is worth sitting with, it is worth being honest about where it came from.

There are 684 verses here, drawn from ten collections — among them the Dhammapada, the Udāna and Itivuttaka, the Sutta Nipāta, the verses of the senior monks and nuns, and the Saṁyutta Nikāya. You are unlikely to meet the same one twice in a sitting.

About 279 of these translations are by Bhikkhu Sujato, published by SuttaCentral and dedicated to the public domain under CC0; the verses of the senior monks and nuns were translated by Bhikkhu Sujato and Jessica Walton. Thanks are owed to them, and to SuttaCentral for making the canon freely reusable. The remaining 405, an older set, came from several translators whose names were not recorded at the time — each is cited to chapter and verse and can be checked, but if you hold rights in any of them, please write to me and I will credit or replace it.
