Why every quote here is cited

Einstein is the most misattributed person on the internet. A great deal of what circulates under his name he never said, and the fakes travel further than the real ones because they are shorter and tidier. So every line on this page is tied to a letter, an interview or a published essay, and anything that could not be traced was left out.

Four you will not find here, because there is no evidence he said them:

  • “Everybody is a genius. But if you judge a fish by its ability to climb a tree…” — no trace before the 2000s.
  • “Insanity is doing the same thing over and over and expecting different results.” — appears in Narcotics Anonymous literature in 1981.
  • “Compound interest is the eighth wonder of the world.” — a bank advertisement, not a physicist.
  • “If you can't explain it simply, you don't understand it well enough.” — attributed to at least three other people first.

The same rule runs the still page, where every verse carries its citation for exactly this reason.
