Two details most copies get wrong

A tile only merges once per move

Slide four 2s left and you get two 4s, never a single 8. Getting this wrong makes the game far too easy and is the commonest bug in a hand-rolled 2048. Each row is collapsed with a flag per tile marking it as already-merged for that move.

A new tile only appears if something moved

Pressing left against a wall where nothing can shift must be a no-op, not a free spawn. Otherwise you can fill the board by pressing a direction that does nothing, which is a way to lose that has nothing to do with the puzzle.

Questions people ask

Is my board saved?
Yes, after every move, in your own browser storage. Close the tab and come back and the board, the score and the move count are where you left them.
How much can I undo?
One move. Enough to take back a misfire, not enough to play the board backwards.
Can I keep going after reaching 2048?
Yes. Reaching 2048 is announced but the run carries on, so 4096 and 8192 are both on the table if the board holds together.
Does it work on a phone?
Yes — swipe in any direction on the board, or use the pad.
