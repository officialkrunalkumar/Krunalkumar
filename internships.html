Internships & Mentorship

Two ways to work with me. Both end with real projects shipped.

A free, selective internship for a handful of standout applicants — and an open, paid mentorship for everyone who wants structured 1:1 coaching. From AI and automation to cybersecurity and full-stack development, the work is real either way. And money never buys the internship: those seats are earned.

The two tracks

Certificates tell the truth

Interns receive a certificate describing the real project work they did. Mentees receive a mentorship-completion certificate. Neither claims employment that didn’t happen. Every document carries a unique ID that anyone can check on the verification page.

Fair pricing, easy exit

The price is on this page, billing is month to month, and you can stop anytime. If the first session isn’t what you expected, say so within the first week and that month is refunded in full.

Frequently asked questions

Is the internship really free?

Yes — ₹0, always. No application fee, no training fee, no hidden charges. Seats are earned through selection, 2–3 per cohort. If anyone asks you to pay for an internship in my name, it is a scam — please report it to me directly.

Can I pay my way into the internship instead?

No. Joining the paid mentorship never converts into an internship title — the two tracks stay separate on purpose. That separation is what keeps both certificates worth something.

How does selection work?

A short application through the form below, then an interview. Commitment and curiosity matter more than what is already on your resume.

What will my certificate actually say?

Interns receive a certificate describing the real project work they shipped. Mentees receive a certificate stating they completed the mentorship program. Neither claims employment or a title that didn’t happen.

Will my college accept this internship for credit?

That is your college’s decision — every institution applies its own internship policy. I provide an offer letter, a completion certificate describing your actual project work, and I am available if your college wants to verify anything. Completion certificates can be checked instantly on the verification page; offer letters I confirm personally by email. To be safe, get approval from your training & placement cell or internship coordinator before you start.

What does the ₹4,999/month mentorship include?

Weekly 1:1 sessions, a personal learning roadmap, portfolio projects we scope together, code review, and interview preparation. Billing is month to month and you can cancel anytime.

How do payments work?

Month to month. Students in India pay ₹4,999/month via UPI; international students pay $120/month via Wise. Once we confirm your plan on WhatsApp, I share the payment QR or account details with you personally. I never post payment links, QR codes, or account details publicly — if you see one claiming to be me, it is fake; please report it to me directly.

What if the first session isn’t what I expected?

Say so within the first seven days of your first month and that month’s fee is refunded in full — no forms, no argument. The details are in the Refund Policy.

Are scholarships available?

Yes — a limited number each month, up to 50% off, based on need and merit. Request one in your application and we’ll discuss it in the interview.

Can I bring my own project idea?

Absolutely. The tracks are open — pick the area that excites you most or bring your own idea, and we will shape the work around where you want to go.
