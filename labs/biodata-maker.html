How it works

Everything is local, and nothing is left behind

The form never submits anywhere — there is no server to submit to. What you type is laid onto the sheet by the page itself, and it stops there: nothing is written to your browser’s storage or anywhere else on the machine. For a sheet carrying a date of birth, an income figure, a home address and a photograph, that is the right default — the biodata cannot be found later on a computer you were only borrowing. The other half of it is that closing or reloading the tab clears the draft, so keep a copy on purpose: “Download data” hands you the whole thing as one small .json file, and “Load data” brings it back. “Clear everything” empties the form and the sheet the moment you press it.

The templates are CSS, not images

Kumkum’s gold frame, Lotus’s florals, Royal’s indigo field — all of it is stylesheet rules and a few lines of inline SVG. No template file is downloaded, nothing is watermarked, and the text stays real text, which is why the printed sheet comes out sharp instead of looking like a stretched JPEG.

Printing is the export

There is no PDF library because the browser already ships a better one: the print engine. The print styles hide this entire site and hand the dialog just the sheet, sized to A4. Choose “Save as PDF” and you get a vector-sharp document; choose a printer and you get paper. Same button, no third-party code touching your details either way.

Every field is optional

Skip gotra, skip income, skip the photo — the sheet renders only what you filled. An empty field does not print as an awkward blank line, and a section you left entirely alone does not even print its heading. What you share is exactly what you chose to type, nothing more.

Questions people ask

Is it really free, with no watermark?
Yes. No account, no watermark, no paid templates, no export limit. The page does the whole job in your browser and the print dialog is the export.
Is my data uploaded anywhere?
No. There is no server behind this page, so there is nothing to upload to. That matters more here than on most tools: a biodata carries a date and time of birth, a home address, an income figure and a photograph on one sheet - exactly the set of details used for identity theft and fake matrimonial profiles. Here they go from the form to the sheet inside your own browser, and nothing leaves it.
How do I get a PDF?
Press Print / Save as PDF, and in the browser's print dialog choose "Save as PDF" as the destination. The page prints only the biodata sheet - no site header, no buttons - sized to A4. A paper printer works the same way.
Will my data still be here tomorrow?
No - and on this form that is exactly the point. A biodata carries a date and time of birth, an income figure, a home address and a photograph on one sheet, and none of it is written to this device. The draft lives in the page's memory for that one session, so closing or reloading the tab clears it, and the shared computer at the print shop keeps nothing after you walk away. The trade is that you have to keep a copy on purpose: press "Download data" before you leave and you get a biodata-data.json file you own, and "Load data" brings it back here or on any other device. "Clear everything" empties the form and the sheet on the spot.
Can I use my own photo?
Yes. The photo is read from your device, resized in the browser to a sensible size, and placed on the sheet. It is never uploaded - it goes from your file picker to the page and nowhere else, and every template is designed to look right with or without one.
Can the biodata be in Hindi or Gujarati?
Yes. The Labels toggle in the toolbar switches every heading and field label printed on the sheet - जन्म तिथि instead of Date of birth - between English, Hindi and Gujarati, while the form you fill stays in English. And whatever you type prints exactly as you typed it, so values written in any language appear on the sheet in that language.
Can I move my biodata to another device?
Yes. Download data (.json) saves everything - every field, the photo, the template and the label language - into one file on your device, and Load data brings it back in any other browser or on any other computer. The file travels however you choose to carry it; the tool itself still sends nothing anywhere.
Does it work offline?
Yes, after the first visit. A small service worker keeps a copy in your browser, so the maker opens, renders and prints without a connection. Fitting, for a tool whose whole point is that no connection is ever used.
Why does a biodata maker need a privacy story?
Because this one page carries a date of birth, a birth time, a home address, an income and a photo: everything an identity thief or a scam matrimonial profile needs, assembled and formatted. Most free biodata sites receive all of it on their servers before showing a watermarked preview. A tool with no server is the only kind that cannot leak it.
