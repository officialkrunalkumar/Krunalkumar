Synth & DJ desk

Load a track from your own machine, then play over it. A sixteen-step drum machine, eight one-shot pads and a playable synth all run through one mixer, so a pattern you build and a line you play land on top of whatever is on the deck. Every sound but your file is generated here — there are no samples to download and nothing to licence.

  • Your file never leaves the browser
  • 16-step drum sequencer
  • Playable synth, QWERTY or on-screen
  • Drums synthesised, not sampled
  • Live oscilloscope

How it works

One mixer, three instruments, no server

The deck, the drum machine and the synth all feed a single gain stage, through a compressor, into an analyser and out. That is why the oscilloscope shows one trace rather than three, and why a pattern you program sits underneath a track rather than beside it.

Your file is read with an object URL and routed through a MediaElementSource. It is never sent anywhere — this site is static and has nowhere to send it. The drums are synthesised the same way the ones on the loud page are: a sine that drops in pitch for the kick, filtered noise for the hats, and three noise bursts nine milliseconds apart for the clap, because one burst is a snare and the smear of repeats is what the ear hears as hands.
