How it makes the picture

Size means frequency

The one thing a word cloud communicates is which words appear most: the biggest word is the most common one. So the text is counted — each word’s size comes from how often it occurs — and the ordinary filler words (“the”, “and”, “of”) that appear in every text are dropped, because they would dominate the cloud while saying nothing about your text in particular. You can turn that off, or add your own words to ignore.

Packed on a spiral, largest first

Each word starts at the centre and walks outward along a spiral until it finds a gap it fits in. The biggest words go first and take the middle; the smaller ones tuck into the spaces left around them. It is the same greedy idea behind the well-known d3-cloud layout, and it is what makes the result look packed rather than scattered.

The shape is a stencil, not a crop

Pick a heart or a star and the words are only placed where they fit inside that outline — the cloud grows into the shape rather than being drawn as a rectangle and then clipped. Rounder shapes hold more words; a thin star will turn some away, and the tool tells you when it does.

Free, and private by construction

There is no account, no watermark and no upload. The counting, the layout and the drawing all happen in your browser, and the PNG is generated and downloaded from the page itself. Your text never travels anywhere — which for a speech draft or unpublished writing is the difference between usable and not.

Questions people ask

Is it really free, with no watermark?
Yes. No account, no watermark, no export limit. The whole thing runs in your browser and the PNG downloads directly from the page.
Is my text uploaded anywhere?
No. The text is counted, laid out and drawn entirely in your browser, and the image is made locally. Nothing is sent to a server - which matters if the text is a draft, a private document or anything unpublished.
Why are words like "the" and "and" missing?
They are filler words that appear in every English text, so they would be the biggest words in every cloud while telling you nothing about this particular text. They are removed by default, but there is a switch to keep them, and you can add your own words to ignore.
Some of my words did not appear - why?
A tight shape can run out of room. The tool places the most frequent words first and tells you if any did not fit; raise the "max words" limit, pick a rounder shape, or use a bigger canvas and they will.
Can I use the image commercially?
The image is made entirely from your own text and downloaded to your machine - there is no licence attached and nothing is uploaded, so what you do with it is up to you.
