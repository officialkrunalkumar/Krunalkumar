Privacy Policy

The short version: this site collects very little, stores nothing on its own servers, and never sells your data. Here is the full picture.

Effective date: 20 August 2026 · Applies to krunalkumar.dpdns.org

Who is responsible for your data

This website is operated by Krunalkumar Shah, an independent consultant based in Gujarat, India, registered with the Ministry of Micro, Small & Medium Enterprises (MSME), Government of India — Udyam Registration No. UDYAM-GJ-23-0050928. For anything related to your personal data, write to krunalkumar@krunalkumar.dpdns.org — I read and answer these messages myself.

What this site collects

Forms. The contact and internship application forms ask for your name, phone number, email address, and a message. This website has no backend and stores none of it: when you press send, your browser opens WhatsApp with the message pre-filled, and it reaches me as an ordinary WhatsApp message. Nothing is saved on this site's servers.

Analytics. The site uses Google Analytics 4 to understand how the site is used — pages visited, approximate location, device and browser type, and interaction events such as clicking the email, WhatsApp, resume, or booking links, submitting a form, verifying a certificate, switching between the light and dark themes, opening the site search, or starting voice or video in the peer-to-peer chat lab. Google Analytics sets cookies to do this.

What analytics never receives. Search terms are not sent. The site search runs entirely in your browser against a file it downloads, and only the number of results a search returned is reported, never what you typed. Nothing you type, paste, or drop into any lab reaches analytics either. In the chat lab, analytics records only that a connection or a call happened: never who with, and never any message, file, or audio and video, all of which travel directly between the two browsers.

The network tools are the exception — and they have to be. Most labs run entirely on your own machine and send nothing anywhere. A handful cannot, because looking something up on the internet is the whole point of the tool. In these, and only these, what you type is sent to the service being queried — not to me, and not to analytics:

  • DNS lookup and email security check — the domain you enter goes to Google Public DNS and, for the DNS tool, Cloudflare DNS.
  • Domain / IP lookup (RDAP) — the domain or address you enter goes to rdap.org.
  • Certificate transparency log — the domain you enter goes to CertSpotter (SSLMate).
  • Password breach check — your password is not sent. It is hashed in your browser and only the first five characters of that hash go to Have I Been Pwned, which matches roughly two thousand hashes at once. The service cannot tell which one was yours.
  • API tester — it sends a request to whatever URL you put in the box. That is the tool. The sample presets point at httpbin.org and the GitHub public API.
  • Peer-to-peer chat and browser leak test — these use WebRTC, which contacts a STUN server (Google and Cloudflare) to discover your network address. On the leak test this happens automatically when the page loads, because showing you what your browser reveals is the purpose of that page.

Direct contact. If you email me, message me on WhatsApp, or book a call through Google Calendar, I receive whatever you choose to share, and it lives in those services under their own privacy policies.

How your data is used

Only to reply to you, to deliver the work or program you asked about, and to understand how the site is used so it can be improved. Your data is never sold or rented, no advertising is shown on this site, and it is shared with no one beyond the service providers listed below. Note that Google Analytics is itself part of Google's advertising infrastructure: alongside its analytics cookies it sets an advertising cookie (_gcl_au) and contacts Google's conversion measurement endpoint. That happens on Google's side, not because anything here is being advertised or sold — but it is data going to an ad platform, and you should know that rather than have to find it in a network log.

Cookies

Every cookie on this site comes from Google Analytics. There are three: _ga and _ga_<id>, which count visits, and _gcl_au, an advertising cookie Google's tag sets on its own. Nothing here reads any of them. You can block or delete them in your browser settings, browse in private mode, use a content blocker, or install Google's opt-out add-on — the site works exactly the same without them, and nothing on it is gated behind accepting them. Separately, the site stores a few preferences in your browser's local storage — your theme choice, and in the labs your saved code and downloaded runtimes. Those never leave your machine and are not cookies; the labs have a button that clears them.

How long data is kept

Correspondence (email, WhatsApp) is kept for as long as needed to work together and to meet legal obligations, then deleted. Analytics data is retained by Google for a limited period per its standard retention settings.

Your rights

Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 — and simply as a matter of decency — you can ask me at any time to tell you what personal data of yours I hold, correct it, or delete it, and you can withdraw consent for further contact. Email krunalkumar@krunalkumar.dpdns.org and I will respond within a reasonable time. If you are not satisfied with my response, you may complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

Children

The internship and mentorship programs are open to students, but if you are under 18, please involve a parent or guardian before sharing personal details or joining a paid program.
