Privacy

Privacy Policy

The short version: this site collects very little, stores nothing on its own servers, and never sells your data. Here is the full picture.

Effective date: 5 August 2026 · Applies to krunalkumar.dpdns.org

Who is responsible for your data

This website is operated by Krunalkumar Shah, an independent consultant based in Gujarat, India. For anything related to your personal data, write to krunalkumar@krunalkumar.dpdns.org — I read and answer these messages myself.

What this site collects

Forms. The contact and internship application forms ask for your name, phone number, email address, and a message. This website has no backend and stores none of it: when you press send, your browser opens WhatsApp with the message pre-filled, and it reaches me as an ordinary WhatsApp message. Nothing is saved on this site's servers.

Analytics. The site uses Google Analytics 4 (loaded both directly and through Google Tag Manager) to understand how the site is used — pages visited, approximate location, device and browser type, and interaction events such as clicking the email, WhatsApp, resume, or booking links, submitting a form, or verifying a certificate. Google Analytics sets cookies to do this.

Direct contact. If you email me, message me on WhatsApp, or book a call through Google Calendar, I receive whatever you choose to share, and it lives in those services under their own privacy policies.

How your data is used

Only to reply to you, to deliver the work or program you asked about, and to understand how the site is used so it can be improved. Your data is never sold or rented, there is no advertising on this site, and it is shared with no one beyond the service providers listed below.

Third-party services

These services process data on this site's behalf, each under its own privacy policy:

Google Analytics & Tag Manager

Site usage measurement. See Google's privacy policy.

WhatsApp (Meta)

Delivery of form submissions and direct messages. See WhatsApp's privacy policy.

Google Calendar

Call bookings. See Google's privacy policy.

Vercel

Site hosting, which involves standard server request logs. See Vercel's privacy policy.

Cookies

The only cookies on this site come from Google Analytics. You can block or delete them in your browser settings, browse in private mode, or install Google's opt-out add-on — the site works exactly the same without them.

How long data is kept

Correspondence (email, WhatsApp) is kept for as long as needed to work together and to meet legal obligations, then deleted. Analytics data is retained by Google for a limited period per its standard retention settings.

Your rights

Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 — and simply as a matter of decency — you can ask me at any time to tell you what personal data of yours I hold, correct it, or delete it, and you can withdraw consent for further contact. Email krunalkumar@krunalkumar.dpdns.org and I will respond within a reasonable time. If you are not satisfied with my response, you may complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

Children

The internship and mentorship programs are open to students, but if you are under 18, please involve a parent or guardian before sharing personal details or joining a paid program.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version appears on this page with an updated effective date. See also the Terms of Service and the Refund Policy.
