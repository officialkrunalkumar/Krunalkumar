Refund Policy

The promises made on the Internships page are public commitments. This page is where they live as formal terms.

Effective date: 5 August 2026 · Applies to krunalkumar.dpdns.org

Paid mentorship — ₹4,999/month (or $120/month internationally)

First-week guarantee. If the first session isn't what you expected, say so within the first seven days of your first month and that month's fee is refunded in full — no forms, no argument. The same applies to scholarship seats, on the amount actually paid.

Cancel anytime. Billing is month to month. Cancelling stops all future charges; the month already underway runs to its end and is not refunded partially, except under the first-week guarantee above.

International payments. If you pay the $120/month international rate, the same guarantee applies. Refunds are returned through Wise after deducting the transfer fees Wise applies, and the amount you receive is subject to the currency conversion rate on the day the refund is processed — so it may differ slightly from the amount you originally paid.

Free internship

The internship costs nothing — no application fee, no training fee, no "registration charge". Money never buys a seat, so there is never anything to refund. If anyone asks you to pay for an internship in my name, it is a scam: please report it to me directly.

Consulting and project work

Each project's written scope states its own payment schedule, and that agreement takes precedence. Where nothing more specific has been agreed: advance payments for work not yet started are refundable in full, and work already delivered or milestones already accepted are not.

Booked sessions

If a paid session doesn't take place because of me, you choose: a rescheduled session or a full refund of that session.

How to request a refund

Email krunalkumar@krunalkumar.dpdns.org or message me on WhatsApp with your name and payment details. I acknowledge requests within 48 hours, and approved refunds are returned to the original payment method within 7 business days — international refunds go back through Wise, net of Wise's transfer fees and subject to currency conversion on the day of processing. See also the Terms of Service and Privacy Policy.
