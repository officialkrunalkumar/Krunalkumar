Cybersecurity

Security reviews of web apps and Linux systems, hardening recommendations, and awareness sessions — grounded in published Linux security research and volunteer casework supporting law enforcement since 2020.

Personal cyber help

Questions, answered straight

Frequently asked questions

How do we start?

With a free intro call — or just a message if you prefer. I listen to the problem first; if I am not the right person for it, I will say so and point you somewhere better.

How do you charge?

You get a written scope with a fixed quote before any work starts — deliverables, timeline, and price. No hourly meters running quietly in the background. Ongoing 1:1 coaching is separate and billed monthly through the mentorship program.

Will you access any device or account I bring you?

Only devices and accounts you own or are explicitly authorized to grant access to. I may ask for proof of ownership, and I decline or stop any work where that isn’t clear — no unlocking, bypassing, or monitoring of anyone else’s device, ever.

Do you work remotely?

Yes — remote-first, with clients across the US, France, Taiwan, and India. Where the work allows it, delivery happens in the open on GitHub so you can verify progress yourself.

Is what I share with you confidential?

Yes. Anything you share for an engagement — code, data, business details — is used only for that engagement and shared with no one. The formal version is in the Terms of Service and Privacy Policy.

What if I’m not satisfied with the work?

The written scope defines what is delivered, and refunds are handled per the Refund Policy — advance payments for work not yet started are refundable in full.
